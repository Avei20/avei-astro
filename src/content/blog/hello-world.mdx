I've been on Nuxt for years. It served me well, but for a personal site that's
mostly writing and a couple of demos, it started to feel heavy. So I tore it down
and started over with Astro.

The draw was simple: real `.mdx` for the blog, islands for the interactive bits,
and static output that costs nothing to host. No client router, no hydration tax
on pages that don't need it.

This post is the first thing rendered by the new pipeline. If you're reading it,
the build worked.
